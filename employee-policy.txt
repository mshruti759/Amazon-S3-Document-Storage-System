================================================================================
                        COMPANY WORKPLACE & CONDUCT POLICY
================================================================================
Document ID: POL-2026-V1
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Applies To: All Full-Time and Part-Time Employees

1. WORKING HOURS & FLEXIBILITY
Core business hours are 09:00 to 17:00 local time. Employees may utilize flexible 
scheduling with manager approval, provided core operational hours (10:00 to 15:00) 
are covered. Remote work arrangements must be documented in the HR portal.

2. TIME OFF & LEAVE REQUESTS
- Paid Time Off (PTO): Standard accrual is 15 days annually. Requests must be 
  submitted via the internal portal at least 5 business days in advance.
- Sick Leave: Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year. Absence exceeding 
  three consecutive business days requires a valid medical note.

3. CODE OF CONDUCT & ETHICS
All team members are expected to maintain high standards of professionalism. 
Harassment, discrimination, or abusive behavior of any kind will result in 
immediate disciplinary action up to and including termination.

4. EQUIPMENT & DATA SECURITY
Company-issued hardware and software must be used strictly for official business. 
Passwords must be changed every 90 days, and multi-factor authentication (MFA) 
is mandatory on all accounts.
